About us

One team for the whole property

Valentine Property & Garden Care is a cleaning and outdoor-care company based in Oslo. We look after homes, hotels, guest houses, restaurants, offices and private estates — so the people who live, work and stay there can enjoy a space that feels cared for.

Instead of hiring one company for the floors, another for the lawn and a third for the hedges, you get a single point of contact who plans the work around your schedule and the Norwegian seasons.

  • Indoors

    Homes, hotels, restaurants and commercial spaces.

  • Outdoors

    Gardens, lawns, hedges, trees and flower beds.

  • Property

    Ongoing care that keeps an estate in good shape.
